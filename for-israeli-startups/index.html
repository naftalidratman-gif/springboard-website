What's in it for you?

Springboard works with Israeli startups and the U.S. enterprises in its network to find real problems that Israeli technology can solve. The startups we bring in have technology that's already proven and ready to deploy, not early-stage concepts. Once a fit is identified, Springboard helps set up a pilot with the enterprise, brings in the right startup, and facilitates the process.
