OUR APPROACH

Why We're Different

Trade missions. Demo days. Pitch decks from Tel Aviv. That's how Israeli innovation has traditionally been introduced to America.

We do things differently. We don't push technology looking for a use case. Instead, we start with a real problem inside a U.S. enterprise, then bring in Israeli technology that's already proven and ready to deploy, guided by the distinctly Israeli mindset of finding the shortest technical path from problem to solution.

Springboarding the United States and Israeli innovation, and the future of everything.
